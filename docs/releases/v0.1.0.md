# Dhoti Dash v0.1.0

The first release: a satirical desi combat-cycling game. Chacha Jee, 72, rides his old black cycle through six errands, and everyone on the road is in his way.

**Play in your browser (phones too):** https://ahab94.github.io/dhoti-dash/

## What's in it
- **Six errands:**
  - dahi before the dairy shuts;
  - the pension window before the bank's lunch break;
  - umpiring the mohalla cricket final;
  - a tiffin run with a rishta aunty on your tail;
  - getting to a wedding through the baraat;
  - the electricity bill on its last date, against Master Jee.
- **Road brawls, last man standing.** Everyone fights everyone. Knock a rival out and he's out of the race, and you take his weapon.
- **Desi weapons:** chhatri, rolled akhbaar, doodh can, kadchi, chappal, belan, jhaadu, Master Jee's scale, and roadside ganna and lauki.
- **Village traffic:** cows who never move, buffaloes, goat herds, hens, stray dogs, bullock carts, sugarcane tractors, vegetable thelas, rickshaws, potholes and speed breakers.
- **Stray dogs** chase anyone. Outrun them, weave, ring the bell, or swing.
- **Breath and back pain.** Pedal hard and you run out of breath. Coast or drink chai to recover. Crashes and hits hurt your back, and at 100 you're retired hurt.
- **Phone controls.** Buttons appear automatically, and Chacha pedals by himself, so steer with one thumb and swing, ring and rest with the other.
- **All music and sound synthesized from scratch:** bhangra dhol, tumbi, harmonium and shehnai, tabla, the cycle bell, a buffalo moo, and cartoon effects.

## Install
- **Browser:** the link above.
- **macOS / Linux:** `curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ahab94/dhoti-dash/main/install.sh | bash`
- **Windows (PowerShell):** `irm https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ahab94/dhoti-dash/main/install.ps1 | iex`
- Or download a zip below.

The builds aren't code-signed:
- **macOS:** right-click the app, then Open, the first time.
- **Windows:** if SmartScreen warns, choose More info, then Run anyway.
