# Dhoti Dash v0.1.4

The rivals get tired too.

**Play in your browser (phones too):** https://ahab94.github.io/dhoti-dash/

## Changed
- **Rivals have breath now, like Chacha.** They pedal until they're tired, then coast to get their breath back ("Ek minute... saans le loon.").
  - Careful riders like Master Jee rest early and never run dry.
  - Reckless ones push until they're panting and crawl at two-thirds speed. That's your moment to overtake, or to swing.
  - Nobody rests in the final stretch: it's a sprint for the line.
- **Sound works on iPhones** (confirmed after v0.1.3's switch to Stream audio).

## Install
- **Browser:** the link above. If your phone shows the old version, reload the page.
- **macOS / Linux:** `curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ahab94/dhoti-dash/main/install.sh | bash`
- **Windows (PowerShell):** `irm https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ahab94/dhoti-dash/main/install.ps1 | iex`
